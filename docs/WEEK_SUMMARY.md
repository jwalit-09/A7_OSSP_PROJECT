# ShellForge Week Summary

## Week 1 – REPL
Interactive command loop.

## Week 2 – Dynamic Memory
malloc(), realloc(), free() and dynamically growing input.

## Week 3 – Parser
strtok() and NULL-terminated argv[].

## Week 4 – Process Execution
fork(), execvp(), waitpid() and error handling.

## Week 5 – Built-ins
cd, pwd, clear, help, exit, env, chdir(), getcwd(), getenv().

## Week 6 – Signals
SIGINT, SIGTSTP, SIGCHLD, sigaction(), signal-safe handlers and zombie cleanup.
